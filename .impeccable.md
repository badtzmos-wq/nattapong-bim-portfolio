## Design Context

### Users
Clients, employers, and BIM/construction professionals reviewing Nattapong Loes-a-nan's project capability, technical workflow, and portfolio evidence. They need to scan quickly, inspect drawings and dashboard visuals, and find contact/CV actions without friction.

### Brand Personality
Professional, technical, calm. The interface should feel precise and construction-ready rather than playful or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, minimal portfolio inspired by a clean architecture/creative portfolio reference, adapted for BIM evidence. Use generous spacing, strong name typography, quiet neutral surfaces, restrained blue accents, and framed drawing previews that preserve image completeness.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize readability and inspection over marketing-style crop.
- Keep card rhythm steady so long BIM titles and tool tags do not disturb the grid.
- Use restrained motion and color to support focus, not decoration.
- Make responsive behavior content-driven, especially for long technical labels.
